How it works

Three steps, one campus loop

From finding a resource to building your reputation — the whole cycle is designed to be safe on both sides of the handoff.

Find a resource

Search the campus shelf or post on the Need Board — matching owners are notified the moment your request goes live.

Borrow, rent, buy, or request

A condition report and a small refundable deposit — sized by your Trust Score — protect both sides before the QR handoff.

Return it safely

Check out with a QR scan, get your deposit released, and watch your Trust Score grow with every on-time return.

Sustainability

Every shared item is one less bought — and one less thrown away.

A campus is thousands of near-identical needs colliding with thousands of idle items. ShareBox closes that loop: sharing cuts student spending, keeps resources in circulation for years instead of weeks, and measurably reduces campus waste.

FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Only students verified through their university email address. Every profile shows its verified campus, so you always know who you’re dealing with — and outsiders can’t get in.

Every borrow starts with a condition report, so both sides agree on the item’s state before handoff. If damage occurs, clear category-specific fee schedules apply — no surprises, no arguments — and the refundable deposit covers it.

A small refundable deposit is held when you borrow or rent, and released automatically once the return is checked in and the condition matches. The higher your Trust Score, the smaller your deposits get.

It’s your reputation on campus, built from on-time returns, item condition, and reviews. It unlocks real benefits: smaller deposits, higher borrowing limits, and priority on the Need Board.

Creating an account, browsing, borrowing, and donating are free. Renting and selling are priced by the owner; deposits are always refundable.
